PATHSOURCE CAREER PLANNING

Connect the dots between education, careers, lifestyle and budget. Exclusive informational interviews with professionals give students unbiased insights into thousands of careers.  

Students can access PathSource either through their mobile device or through a browser on the web. iPhone users will need to download the app by searching for "PathSource" in the Apple App Store or by following this link. Students without iPhones can visit the PathSource site on the web and create an account (click "Register" at the top righthand corner of the home page). When they create their account, they'll need to enter the Merritt College access code: MER658
