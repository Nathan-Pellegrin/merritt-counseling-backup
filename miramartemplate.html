Transfer to UC San Diego

Minimum Transfer Admission Requirements

o   Complete a minimum of 60 UC-transferable semester units.

o   Complete the following 7-course pattern:

o        Two UC-transferable courses in English composition (UC-E)

o        One UC-transferable course in mathematics (UC-M)

o        Four UC-transferable courses in at least two of the following areas:
a) arts & humanities (UC-H)
b) behavioral & social sciences (UC-B)
c) biological & physical sciences (UC-S)

Visit www.assist.org for the list of all UC-transferable Miramar College courses.

Note: students who complete the IGETC pattern will meet these requirements.

o   Earn a minimum 2.4 cumulative UC-transferable GPA. (Note: a significantly higher UC-transferable GPA is typically required for admission to UCSD).

o   Be in good standing at the last college or university attended.

Completing the minimum transfer admission requirements does not assure admission. Each UC campus evaluates all applications for transfer admission using a comprehensive review process. UCSD also uses additional selection criteria to evaluate transfer applicants.

Click here to learn more about UC paths to transfer or visit www.uctransfer.universityofcalifornia.edu.

 

Recommended Transfer Coursework

o   General Education: Complete the IGETC pattern, unless you plan to attend Revelle or Eleanor Roosevelt Colleges. If you are only applying to these colleges, complete the 7-course pattern required for minimum transfer eligibility and the General Education / Breadth requirements for your college as listed on www.assist.org.

Click here for more information about UC general education requirements.

o   Preparation for Major: Complete the preparation for major courses / lower division requirements for your university major as listed on www.assist.org.

Click here for more information about UC preparation for major courses.

 

How to Apply & Transfer

o   Submit UC Application at www.universityofcalifornia.edu.

For fall admission, apply November 1 to 30 of the year prior to transfer.

UCSD does not accept applications for winter or spring terms.

o   Complete 60 UC-transferable units and the 7-course pattern required for minimum transfer eligibility by the end of the spring term prior to fall transfer, or by the end of the fall term prior to winter or spring transfer.

o   Follow instructions from the university for submitting official transcripts, enrollment confirmation and deposit, and other documents and information prior to transfer. Visit the UCSD prospective student webpage for more information.

 

Top Tips for Transfers

o   Select a major early in your community college career.

o   See a counselor as soon as possible to develop a transfer education plan.

o    Sign a Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG) agreement to get guaranteed university admission (most but not all students are eligible). Visit the UC TAG webpage for the application, eligibility criteria, and other information.

o   If you are a high school or first semester college student, enroll in the UCSD UniversityLink guaranteed admission program through the Miramar College Transfer Center in room B-203.
